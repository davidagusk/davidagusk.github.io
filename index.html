Hi! My name is David. I'm a Full Stack Web Developer and digital visual artist. I love crafting and building interactive experiences on the web. My passion for web development began when I started uploading my digital concept artworks and wanted a custom space to showcase them online.

Instead of just creating an online gallery, I found myself fascinated by the process of developing and designing for the web. That curiosity led me deeper into coding, UI/UX, and the creative possibilities of interactive websites.

These days, my main focus is designing fun, clean, and thoughtful web interfaces. I love coding from scratch and enjoy transforming ideas into functional, beautiful websites right in the browser.
